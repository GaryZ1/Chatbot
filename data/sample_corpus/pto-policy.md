# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Owner: People Team. Effective date: 2026-01-01.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue **1.75 days** of PTO per month (21 days per year).
Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata to their contracted hours.

## Carry-over

Up to **5 unused days** may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on
March 31 if they are not used.

## Requesting time off

Submit PTO requests in the HR portal at least **two weeks** in advance for absences longer than
three days. Managers approve or decline within 3 business days.

## Public holidays

Public holidays are not deducted from PTO. The holiday calendar is published per region (EMEA, AMER, APAC).
