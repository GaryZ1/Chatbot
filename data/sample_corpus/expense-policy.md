# Travel and Expense Policy

Owner: Finance. Effective date: 2025-09-15.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance during business travel is **USD 75** (USD 100 in New York, London and Tokyo).
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of
6 hours or longer. Book flights through the travel portal at least 14 days in advance when possible.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports within **30 days** of the expense date with itemized receipts for any item
above USD 25. Reports are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle after approval.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards (product code CC-2041) may only be used for business expenses. Personal charges must
be repaid within 10 days.
