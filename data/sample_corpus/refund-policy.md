# Refund Policy

Owner: Customer Operations. Effective date: 2026-03-01. Version 3.

## 1. Eligibility

Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of purchase for any annual plan.
Monthly plans are refundable within **7 days** of the most recent charge.
Refunds are not available for usage-based overage charges.

## 2. How to request a refund

1. Open the billing portal and choose **Request refund**.
2. Select the invoice and give a reason code.
3. Support confirms the request within 2 business days.

## 3. Processing times

| Payment method | Processing time |
|---|---|
| Credit card | 5–10 business days |
| Bank transfer (ACH/SEPA) | 3–5 business days |
| Invoice / PO | Credit note issued within 1 business day |

## 4. Exceptions

Enterprise contracts with a negotiated master services agreement (MSA) follow the refund terms in
that MSA. Where the MSA is silent, this policy applies.
